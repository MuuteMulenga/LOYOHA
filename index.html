About Us

About LOYOHA Investments

LOYOHA Investments Limited is a dynamic Zambian company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2017. We are a diversified enterprise operating across multiple sectors including construction, agriculture, manufacturing, and wholesale trade.

Our core businesses focus on providing essential services to schools, offices, and institutions across Zambia. We specialize in food supply chains, stationery and educational materials, and comprehensive construction services. With a registered capital of K20,000 and a commitment to excellence, we deliver quality services and products to our clients across Zambia.

Our team of experienced professionals ensures that every project meets the highest standards of quality and professionalism. We pride ourselves on being a 100% Zambian-owned company, dedicated to contributing to the nation's development through sustainable business practices.
